OTHTECH Leave Portal — Working Hours + Overtime Update

WEBSITE
- 2 sections: "Leaves" aur "Working Hours"
- Working Hours: Daily / Weekly / Monthly / Overtime
- Overtime:
    * Balance card (agli extra leave tak kitna overtime baqi)
    * "Overtime history": sirf is hafte + pichle hafte, din-ba-din + Week total
    * "Earned leaves": har extra leave ki alag entry (date, 8h used, month) — permanent

GOOGLE SHEET (3 naye tabs, khud ban jate hain; purane tabs waise hi)
- Overtime Months   (PERMANENT) har mahina: Carried In, Overtime, Short, Total,
                    Extra Leaves, Carried Forward, Status (Running / Final)
- Earned Leaves     (PERMANENT) har extra leave ki ek row
- Overtime History  sirf aakhri 2 haftay — har sync par purana hafta khud hat jata hai
                    (cleanup SIRF is tab par hoti hai)
- Raw Log, Daily Summary, Monthly Summary, Issues, Employees, Leaves, Extra Leaves: koi change nahi

HISAAB
- Worked = check in se check out. Break nahi ginta, namaz kaam mein ginti hai
  (9h office = 8h kaam + 1h break)
- Required = 8h har Mon–Sat poora din (Sunday off: saara kaam overtime)
- Balance = Carried in + Overtime − Short
- Jis din balance 8h ho: 1 extra leave, 8h minus. Baqi aage (agle mahine bhi).
  Misal: 11h -> 1 leave + 3h aage
- Mahina khatam hone ke 3 din baad (SETTLE_GRACE_DAYS) mahina "Final" ho kar lock.
  Final mahine aur us ki leaves script kabhi nahi badalti. Agla mahina Final
  "Carried Forward" se shuru hota hai, is liye balance kabhi reset nahi hota.
- Safety: agar kisi sync mein koi Final row badalne lage to script ruk jati hai, kuch nahi likhti.
